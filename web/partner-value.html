01 / THE STORY

The useful angle for a value investor

Berkshire’s SEC filing illustrates why a company that looks like one ticker can be many distinct operating businesses. Understanding its mix of subsidiaries and segment exposure comes before any attempt to value it.

PROPOSED VIDEO HOOK

“One ticker, completely different businesses: how to research Berkshire without getting fooled by a single multiple.”

Questions for the audience
  1. Why does the filing separate insurance and other operations from railroad, utilities and energy?
  2. Which businesses are regulated, and why does that matter for estimating future cash flows?
  3. What do the segment and risk disclosures tell you that a headline valuation multiple doesn't?
