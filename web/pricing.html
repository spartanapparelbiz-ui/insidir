Questions

Before you subscribe.

Anything else? Write to hello@aethric.llc.

Is there a trial?

Yes: 7 days of the whole product for $1, once per account. If you do not cancel before it ends, the plan renews at $39 a month. There is no free tier.

Why do I create an account before I pay?

Your subscription belongs to your Insidir account. You create the account first, then subscribe on the next page through Stripe Checkout.

When am I charged?

$1 when you complete checkout. $39 when the 7-day trial ends, then every month from that day until you cancel. Cancel before the trial ends and nothing more is charged.

How do I cancel, and what happens then?

Open Billing in your account and press Cancel subscription: one step, no call or email. No further charges are made, a trial ends without the $39 charge, and your access continues to the end of the period you have paid for. After that, the product asks you to subscribe again; your account and saved research stay until you delete the account.

Can I get a refund?

Cancelling stops future charges; payments already made are not refunded automatically. You can ask for a refund by emailing hello@aethric.llc, and we review each request. Any refund or cancellation right that applicable law gives you applies in addition.

Does Insidir see my card?

No. You enter payment details on Stripe's checkout page, and Stripe processes the payment. Insidir keeps only your Stripe customer and subscription references, the subscription's status and its current period, never your card number. See the privacy policy.

Are prices live?

No. Prices are delayed. Insidir is built for researching companies, not for timing trades. See Data & sources.

Is Insidir investment advice?

No. Insidir is research software. It does not execute trades, hold money or make personalized recommendations. See Why Insidir.
