Frequently asked questions.

What data does insidir. include?

Insidir currently focuses on imported SEC Form 4 ownership records, reporting people and transactions, linked to their original filings. Broader political and financial information is planned where access and licensing permit.

Can Insidir execute trades automatically?

Not today. You can use Insidir research to inform decisions made in your own brokerage account. Connected portfolios and user-authorized trading automation are future capabilities dependent on broker support and required approvals; they are not included in a current subscription.

How often is the data updated?

The engine supports scheduled SEC ingestion. Actual freshness depends on source availability and a healthy import process. Insidir distinguishes the transaction date from the publication date so you can check the timing.

Can I upgrade or cancel at any time?

Plans are monthly. Once checkout is configured, subscribers can manage or cancel through the billing portal. Research access depends on the current plan and available imported data.
