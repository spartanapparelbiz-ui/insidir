THE IMPORTANT DETAILS

Questions, answered.

Do I need a brokerage account?

No. You can research companies and official filings before ever connecting a brokerage. Manually entered holdings are optional.

Where does the data come from?

Insidir uses imported SEC Form 4 ownership records and, where available, official company submissions and XBRL financial facts. All information should be verified at SEC EDGAR.

Does Insidir recommend or execute trades?

No. Insidir currently offers public-record research, not personalized investment advice or trading execution.

When is Pro billing available?

Subscription checkout will be enabled only after Insidir's Stripe configuration and entitlements are verified in production. There is no charge before that.
