The whole form, inside Insidir.

A filing opens in Insidir laid out in the form's own boxes and tables, with every footnote, and the reported lines in plain words above it. The original on SEC.gov is always linked.

  1. 1

    Trade

    The day the insider bought, sold, received or exercised.

  2. 2

    Filing

    A Form 4 is generally due within two business days of the trade.

  3. 3

    Insidir

    Read from the SEC, shown with both dates and the link to the filing.

Every filing is stored once under its accession number. Amendments are marked as amendments, never merged. The library holds the filings Insidir has imported, starting from recent ones; it is not a complete historical archive.

A signal score for every open-market trade.

Low, Medium or High: how much the filing says, worked out by fixed rules and explained in one sentence. It describes the filing; it is not a forecast or a recommendation.

  • Buy or sale

    An open-market purchase says more than a sale. Pre-scheduled 10b5-1 sales are labelled and score low.

  • Size

    How large the trade is against what the insider held before it.

  • Others buying

    Whether other insiders at the same company bought around the same time.

  • First in a while

    Whether this is the insider's first purchase at the company in a long time.

  • After a fall

    Whether the insider bought after the stock had fallen.

  • Track record

    How this insider's past purchases went in the months after them.

Awards, gifts, tax withholding and option exercises are routine and say so instead of scoring.

Follow once. Hear about it when it is filed.

Watch a stock or follow an insider, and new filings that match arrive in Alerts.

  1. 1

    Save and follow

    Watch a stock from its page and follow an insider from theirs. Remove either the same way.

  2. 2

    Checked and matched

    New Form 4 filings are imported about every 2 minutes, and watched companies' other SEC filings on their own schedule, then matched to what you follow.

  3. 3

    In Alerts

    Each match arrives in Alerts in one sentence with its signal score, marked unread. Open it to go to the stock, or archive it, with an Undo.

Important alerts cover purchases, purchases by several insiders, large sales and company disclosures. Device notifications are opt-in, and delivery can be delayed. Filings from before you followed are not alerted. Insidir sends no email.
