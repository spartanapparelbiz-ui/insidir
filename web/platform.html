Company reports

One report for every listed company.

Search any company in the SEC's official ticker list and open its report: the delayed price chart, key facts, reported financials, filings, insider activity, people and news, each with its source.

Calculated values show their inputs.

Hover a calculated fact to see what it was built from.

Market cap
price × shares outstanding
Enterprise value
market cap + total debt − cash and short-term investments
P/E ratio
price ÷ trailing diluted EPS
TTM
latest fiscal year + current year to date − prior year to date
  • Financial statements from XBRL, in US GAAP and in IFRS for 20-F and 40-F filers.
  • Financial values carry their form, their period and a link to the filing they were reported in.
  • The insider tab reads the company's 16 most recent Form 4 and 4/A filings from EDGAR when you open it.
  • The news tab lists 8-K announcements with their item descriptions, and headlines that link to the publisher.
  • Calculated values are skipped for foreign issuers whose listed shares may be ADRs, and missing values are shown as missing.

SEC filings

A filing library you can check line by line.

Insidir imports Form 4 ownership filings from the SEC, parses every transaction and keeps the link to the original document. Search the library by company or ticker and open any filing in the viewer.

  1. 01

    Read from the SEC

    New filings come from the SEC's current-filings feed, requested with a declared identity and below the SEC's rate limit.

  2. 02

    Parsed from the XML

    Each filing is read from its ownership XML: who filed, the security, the code, the date, the shares and the reported price.

  3. 03

    Kept once

    Every filing is stored once under its accession number. Amendments are marked as amendments, never merged.

  4. 04

    Opened in the viewer

    The filing viewer lists the parsed transactions and links straight to the document on EDGAR.

The library holds the filings Insidir has imported, starting from recent filings; it is not a complete historical archive. Company reports read each company's own filing history from EDGAR directly.

Insider activity & people

Purchases and sales, kept apart.

Form 4 gives every kind of transaction a letter code. Insidir counts only open-market purchases and sales as buying and selling; awards, exercises, tax withholding and gifts are shown by name and never counted as trades.

  • POpen-market purchaseCounted as buying
  • SOpen-market saleCounted as selling
  • AGrant or awardShown, not counted
  • MOption exerciseShown, not counted
  • FTax withholdingShown, not counted
  • GGiftShown, not counted
  • DDisposition to the issuerShown, not counted
  • JOther acquisition or dispositionShown, not counted

What each transaction shows

  • The reporting person and their role at the company
  • The transaction code, in words
  • Shares, the reported price and the value they imply
  • Shares held after the transaction, as reported
  • Derivative trades and amendments, labelled as such
  • The filing itself, one click away on EDGAR

Watchlist & alerts

Watch a company. See what its insiders file.

Save tickers to a private watchlist. Alerts gathers the Form 4 transactions at those companies published in the last 30 days, inside the app.

  1. 01

    Save a ticker

    Add a company to your watchlist from its report or from the watchlist page. Remove it the same way.

  2. 02

    Matched to filings

    Insidir matches imported Form 4 transactions to your watchlist by ticker.

  3. 03

    Listed in Alerts

    Matches from the last 30 days, newest first, each with the filer, the kind of transaction, the value and the source.

Alerts are put together when you open them. Insidir does not send email or push notifications.

Research journal

Write down why. Keep it private.

On the research desk, give each company a status and keep notes beside the evidence. The journal is private to your account and never places an order.

  • Researching

    You are reading the filings and checking the numbers.

  • Watching

    You have a view and are waiting for the next filing.

  • Passed

    You looked and decided against it, with the reason written down.

Notes  Up to 1,500 characters per company: the sources you checked, the dates to verify, what would change your mind.

On the research desk

  • Selected 8-K, 10-K, 10-Q, proxy and ownership filings for the company
  • Four reported XBRL measures, where the company reports them
  • A chronological brief of filings and imported Form 4 trades
  • Your status and notes, saved per company and deleted with your account
