Core capabilities

Four capabilities. On every profile.

Built into company and person profiles, research results and your briefing, not separate apps. Plain words first; the filing detail and the source one step further.

  • Connections

    Who is connected

    Companies, executives, institutional holders and their disclosures on an interactive map from Form 4 records, with zoom, filters, an as-of date and a table view.

  • Changes

    What actually changed

    New insider transactions, amendments, role changes, results against a year earlier and 8-K events, each headlined in plain words with the figures as filed and its footnotes kept.

  • Autopilot

    Follow once

    Follow a company or reporting person and choose important updates, every filing, a daily or weekly summary, or pause. Updates come from supported public records.

  • Evidence

    Verify the figure

    Every change opens its source: the SEC document, the field, the period, the value as filed and how anything derived was calculated.

Company reports

One report per company.

Delayed prices, reported financials, filings, insiders and people. Each sourced.

Calculated values show their inputs.

Hover a calculated fact to see what it was built from.

Market cap
price × shares outstanding
Enterprise value
market cap + total debt − cash and short-term investments
P/E ratio
price ÷ trailing diluted EPS
TTM
latest fiscal year + current year to date − prior year to date
  • Financial statements from XBRL, in US GAAP and in IFRS for 20-F and 40-F filers.
  • Financial values carry their form, their period and a link to the filing they were reported in.
  • The insider tab reads the company's 16 most recent Form 4 and 4/A filings from EDGAR when you open it.
  • The news tab lists 8-K announcements with their item descriptions, and headlines that link to the publisher.
  • Calculated values are skipped for foreign issuers whose listed shares may be ADRs, and missing values are shown as missing.

SEC filings

A verifiable filing library.

Form 4 filings, parsed line by line, linked to EDGAR.

  1. 01

    Read from the SEC

    New filings come from the SEC's current-filings feed, requested with a declared identity and below the SEC's rate limit.

  2. 02

    Parsed from the XML

    Each filing is read from its ownership XML: who filed, the security, the code, the date, the shares and the reported price.

  3. 03

    Kept once

    Every filing is stored once under its accession number. Amendments are marked as amendments, never merged.

  4. 04

    Opened in the viewer

    The filing viewer lists the parsed transactions and links straight to the document on EDGAR.

The library holds the filings Insidir has imported, starting from recent filings; it is not a complete historical archive. Company reports read each company's own filing history from EDGAR directly.

Monitoring & alerts

Disclosure monitoring.

Watchlists and followed people, matched to new SEC filings on a schedule.

  1. 01

    Save and follow

    Add a company to your watchlist from its report, and follow a reporting person from their file. Remove either the same way.

  2. 02

    Checked and matched

    Form 4 filings are imported about every 2 minutes and company reports (8-K, 6-K) on their own schedule, then matched to what you follow.

  3. 03

    In your inbox

    New matches appear in Alerts, marked unread, with the filer, the transaction, the value and the source; Alerts also lists the last 30 days of matching activity.

Push and email are opt-in; delivery can be delayed. Filings from before you followed are not alerted. No marketing email.

Research journal

A private research journal.

A status and notes per company, beside the evidence.

  • Researching

    You are reading the filings and checking the numbers.

  • Watching

    You have a view and are waiting for the next filing.

  • Passed

    You looked and decided against it, with the reason written down.

Notes  Up to 1,500 characters per company: the sources you checked, the dates to verify, what would change your mind.

  • A chronological brief of filings and imported Form 4 trades
  • Your status and notes, saved per company and deleted with your account
