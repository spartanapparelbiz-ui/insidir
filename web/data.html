Each source, named.

Five come from the SEC and carry the filings themselves. The others add delayed prices, headlines, company profiles and logos.

  1. SEC EDGAR filings

    Official · U.S. SEC

    EDGAR ↗

    Each company's filing history: annual and quarterly reports, current reports on Form 8-K, proxy statements and ownership filings, with the original documents.

    Insidir requests it with a declared identity and keeps its requests below the SEC's fair-access limit of ten per second.

    Used in
    Stock pages (Filings), the filing view, alerts, Intelligence, the research desk
    Freshness
    Watched companies' new filings are checked about every 30 minutes; a filed document, which never changes, is read once and kept

  2. Form 4 ownership reports

    Official · U.S. SEC

    EDGAR ↗

    What officers, directors and 10% owners report buying, selling, receiving and exercising, parsed from the filed ownership XML, footnotes included.

    New filings come from the SEC's current-filings feed. EDGAR's own daily index of Form 4 filings is read to catch anything the feed missed.

    Used in
    Today, stock pages, insiders and their track records, signal scores, alerts, the portfolio
    Freshness
    New filings checked about every 2 minutes when automatic import is enabled; the daily index every hour

  3. SEC XBRL company facts

    Official · U.S. SEC

    EDGAR APIs ↗

    Financial statement values exactly as the company tagged them, with the period, the form and the filing date. Insidir reads US GAAP and, for 20-F and 40-F filers, IFRS.

    Used in
    Intelligence (results against a year earlier), the research desk

  4. Form 13F holdings

    Official · U.S. SEC

    EDGAR ↗

    The quarterly holdings that institutional investment managers report, read from EDGAR. Reported up to 45 days after each quarter ends, so they describe the past.

    Used in
    The Funds tab on stock pages, Mirror

  5. SEC company ticker list

    Official · U.S. SEC

    The list ↗

    The SEC's own mapping from ticker to company identifier (CIK). Insidir resolves tickers through it, so a stock page belongs to the right company.

    Used in
    Search, stock pages
    Freshness
    Refreshed daily

  6. Yahoo Finance

    Delayed prices · third party

    Yahoo Finance ↗

    Delayed daily closing prices, stored by Insidir, plus headlines from the ticker's public news feed. Headlines link to the publisher; Insidir copies no article text.

    Prices are delayed, are not an official record of trades and are not meant for trading.

    Used in
    Price charts, insiders' track records, headlines
    Freshness
    Closes downloaded about once a day per stock; headlines kept 15 minutes

  7. Wikidata

    CC0 · public domain

    Wikidata ↗

    Founding year, employee count, official website and headquarters. A profile is matched to the company only by its SEC identifier (Wikidata property P5531).

    Used in
    The Company tab on stock pages
    Freshness
    Kept for 24 hours

  8. Wikipedia

    CC BY-SA 4.0

    Wikipedia ↗

    The short company description, taken from the summary of the company's article.

    Used in
    Company descriptions
    Freshness
    Kept for 24 hours

  9. Company logos

    License per file

    Wikimedia Commons ↗

    Logos come from public logo services, Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons, SEC filings and companies' own websites. Insidir's server checks each image and serves it from insidir.com; a company without a confirmed logo shows its ticker's initials.

    Used in
    Logos beside each stock

How old a number can be.

Insidir checks each source on a schedule and keeps a copy of what it reads for a short time. The longest it waits, by kind of data:

  1. About every 2 minutes

    • New Form 4 filings, when automatic import is enabled

  2. 15 minutes

    • Headlines

  3. About every 30 minutes

    • Other SEC filings of watched companies

  4. Every hour

    • EDGAR's daily Form 4 index, checked against the import

  5. About once a day

    • Daily closing prices, per stock
    • The SEC company ticker list
    • Wikidata and Wikipedia profiles

  6. Kept

    • Filed documents, which never change

Prices are delayed by the provider before Insidir reads them; they are never live. A source that cannot be reached is reported as unavailable rather than filled in.

Sources Insidir leaves alone.

Some data is popular with investing apps. Insidir does not ingest it until its legal and licensing questions are settled, and some of it never.

  • Disabled

    Congressional disclosures

    House and Senate financial disclosures are not scraped, ingested or shown pending legal review.

  • Disabled

    Social media

    No posts are collected from X, Truth Social or any other service without authorized access.

  • Disabled

    Crypto wallets

    No wallet is attributed to a public figure without strong, verifiable evidence.

  • Never

    Invented data

    No made-up figure fills a gap, and no rating or recommendation is published. AI-assisted wording, where switched on, is labelled and cites its records.

What the record cannot tell you.

Public data has gaps. These are the ones that matter most when you read Insidir.

  • The filing library starts from the filings Insidir has imported; it is not a complete historical archive.
  • The SEC notes that its ticker list may be incomplete, so a company may not be found by ticker.
  • Not every company reports every measure in XBRL, and values can be restated in later filings.
  • A Form 4 is filed after the trade, generally within two business days; amendments can repeat or correct earlier reports.
  • 13F holdings are reported weeks after the quarter they describe.
  • Prices are delayed, can be unavailable and are not an official record of trades.
  • Wikidata and Wikipedia are edited by volunteers and can lag or be wrong; a company with no SEC identifier on Wikidata has no profile.
  • Any source can be briefly unavailable. Pages say so rather than estimate.
