Insidir / Data & sources

Where every number comes from.

Insidir reads public records from the SEC and a short list of named public sources. This page lists each one: what Insidir uses it for, how fresh it is, how it is licensed and what it cannot tell you.

Sources

Eight sources, each one named.

Four come from the SEC and carry the filings themselves. The other four add delayed prices, headlines and company profiles, and are credited wherever they appear.

  1. SEC EDGAR submissions

    Official · U.S. SEC

    EDGAR ↗

    Each company's filing history: annual and quarterly reports, current reports on Form 8-K, proxy statements and ownership filings, with the original documents.

    Insidir requests it with a declared identity and keeps its requests below the SEC's fair-access limit of ten per second.

    Used in
    Company reports (SEC filings, News), the research desk
    Freshness
    Read when you open a report; kept for up to 15 minutes

  2. SEC XBRL company facts

    Official · U.S. SEC

    EDGAR APIs ↗

    Financial statement values exactly as the company tagged them, with the period, the form and the filing date. Insidir reads US GAAP and, for 20-F and 40-F filers, IFRS.

    Trailing-twelve-month figures, market cap, enterprise value and P/E are calculated from these values and show their inputs.

    Used in
    Financials, key facts, the research desk
    Freshness
    Read when you open a report; kept for up to 15 minutes

  3. Form 4 ownership reports

    Official · U.S. SEC

    EDGAR ↗

    What officers, directors and 10% owners report buying, selling, receiving and exercising, parsed from the filed ownership XML.

    Insidir uses them two ways: an imported library of recent filings from the SEC's current-filings feed, and each company's own recent filings, read from EDGAR when you open its report.

    Used in
    SEC filings, insider activity, people, alerts, portfolio
    Freshness
    Library: checked every 15 minutes when automatic import is enabled. Reports: summaries kept 15 minutes, filed documents 24 hours

  4. SEC company ticker list

    Official · U.S. SEC

    The list ↗

    The SEC's own mapping from ticker to company identifier (CIK). Insidir resolves every search and report through it, never through a fuzzy name match.

    Used in
    Search, company reports
    Freshness
    Kept for 24 hours

  5. Yahoo Finance

    Delayed prices · third party

    Yahoo Finance ↗

    Delayed price history for six ranges and the latest session quote, plus headlines from the ticker's public news feed. Headlines link to the publisher; Insidir copies no article text.

    Prices are delayed, are not an official record of trades and are not meant for trading.

    Used in
    Price chart, market cap, enterprise value, P/E, news headlines
    Freshness
    Prices kept 1 to 60 minutes depending on the range; headlines 15 minutes

  6. Wikidata

    CC0 · public domain

    Wikidata ↗

    Founding year, latest employee count, official website, logo, leadership and founders. A profile is matched to the company only by its SEC identifier (Wikidata property P5531).

    Used in
    Company profiles, people in company reports
    Freshness
    Kept for 24 hours

  7. Wikipedia

    CC BY-SA 4.0

    Wikipedia ↗

    The short description at the top of a company report, taken from the summary of the company's article and credited with a link to it and to the license.

    Used in
    Company descriptions
    Freshness
    Kept for 24 hours

  8. Wikimedia Commons

    License per file

    Commons ↗

    Company logos and published photographs of executives, loaded from Wikimedia's servers. Each file is used under its own license, and no portrait is generated or guessed.

    Used in
    Company logos, people in company reports
    Freshness
    Loaded from Wikimedia when shown

Freshness

How old a number can be.

Insidir keeps a copy of what it reads for a short time before asking the source again. The longest it waits, by kind of data:

  1. 1min
    • Prices, one-day range
  2. 5min
    • Prices, one-week range
  3. 10min
    • Prices, one-month range
  4. 15min
    • Prices, three months and one year
    • Company filings and XBRL facts
    • Insider activity in reports
    • 8-K announcements and headlines
    • Form 4 import, when enabled
  5. 1hour
    • Prices, five-year range
  6. 24hours
    • SEC company ticker list
    • Wikidata and Wikipedia profiles
    • Filed Form 4 documents, which never change

Prices are delayed by the provider before Insidir reads them; they are never live. A source that cannot be reached is reported as unavailable rather than filled in.

Not used

Sources Insidir leaves alone.

Some data is popular with investing apps. Insidir does not ingest it until its legal and licensing questions are settled, and some of it never.

  • Disabled

    Congressional disclosures

    House and Senate financial disclosures are not scraped, ingested or shown pending legal review.

  • Disabled

    Social media

    No posts are collected from X, Truth Social or any other service without authorized access.

  • Disabled

    Crypto wallets

    No wallet is attributed to a public figure without strong, verifiable evidence.

  • Never

    Generated text

    No summaries, ratings or signals written by AI, and no invented data to fill a gap.

Limitations

What the record cannot tell you.

Public data has gaps. These are the ones that matter most when you read Insidir.

  • The filing library starts from the filings Insidir has imported; it is not a complete historical archive.
  • The SEC notes that its ticker list may be incomplete, so a company may not be found by ticker.
  • Not every company reports every measure in XBRL, and values can be restated in later filings.
  • A Form 4 is filed after the trade, generally within two business days; amendments can repeat or correct earlier reports.
  • Prices are delayed, can be unavailable and are not an official record of trades.
  • Market cap, enterprise value and P/E are not calculated for foreign issuers whose listed shares may be ADRs.
  • Wikidata and Wikipedia are edited by volunteers and can lag or be wrong; a company with no SEC identifier on Wikidata has no profile.
  • Any source can be briefly unavailable. Pages say so rather than estimate.
