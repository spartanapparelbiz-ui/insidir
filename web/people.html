    What a Form 4 tells you

    Who
    The reporting person: their name, SEC CIK and role at the company, such as director, officer or owner of more than 10%.
    What
    The transaction: a code (P purchase, S sale, A award, M option exercise) with the shares and the price.
    When
    Two dates: the trade date, and the filing date, generally within two business days of it.
    Source
    SEC EDGAR: a filing number that opens the original document.
