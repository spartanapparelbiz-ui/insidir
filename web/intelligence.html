        Insidir Intelligence works from public records: SEC Form 4 ownership reports, 8-K and 6-K company reports, EDGAR filing lists and Form 13F holdings reports. Filings can arrive days after the events they describe; a record Insidir has not indexed is not evidence that nothing happened. Plain-English summaries marked as AI-written are generated from the cited records and checked against them before they are shown.
