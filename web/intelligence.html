What Mirror is not

  • No trade copying. Mirror does not copy, follow or replicate anyone's trades, and cannot place orders.
  • Not live. Form 4 is due two business days after a trade and is often later; every line shows the delay.
  • No portfolio model. It does not estimate holdings, returns or performance.
