01 / Change or cancel

In Stripe's billing portal

Manage billing opens Stripe's secure portal, where you can update your card, download invoices or cancel. Cancellation stops renewal and keeps access through the end of the period you've already paid for.

Cancels billing first

Deleting your account from Account cancels the subscription with Stripe before anything is removed, so no further payments are taken.
