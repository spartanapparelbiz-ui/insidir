Account deletion

Delete your account

Deleting your account first cancels any Insidir subscription with Stripe, so no further payments are taken; if Stripe can't confirm the cancellation, nothing is deleted. It then revokes any read-only brokerage connection and removes your login, every session, your watchlist, research journal, followed people and manually entered holdings. This cannot be undone.
