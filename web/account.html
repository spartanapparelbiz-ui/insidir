Delete your account

Deleting your account first cancels any Insidir subscription with Stripe, so no further payments are taken; if Stripe can't confirm the cancellation, nothing is deleted. It then revokes any read-only brokerage connection and removes your login, every session, your watchlist, research journal, followed people, manually entered holdings, alerts and alert settings. Records the law requires us to keep (your subscription consents) are kept as the privacy policy explains. This cannot be undone.
