What's included

  1. 01

    Company reports

    Any SEC-listed ticker: a delayed price chart, key facts, XBRL financial statements, filings, Form 4 insider activity, people and 8-K announcements.

  2. 02

    Research desk

    SEC submissions and reported financial facts for a ticker, each linked to its source, beside your private research journal.

  3. 03

    Filings and insider activity

    Imported Form 4 filings and the transactions they report, with links to the original EDGAR documents.

  4. 04

    People

    A directory of reporting insiders. Follow the people you want to keep track of.

  5. 05

    Watchlist and alerts

    Save tickers and see recent insider activity on them, inside the app.

  6. 06

    Portfolio

    Record your holdings by hand and see related SEC activity. No brokerage connection required.
