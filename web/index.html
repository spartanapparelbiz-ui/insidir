Frequently asked questions.

What data does insidir. include?

Insidir starts with imported SEC Form 4 ownership filings, reporting people, companies and transaction dates. Wider political, institutional and government records are planned where access and commercial use are permitted.

How often is the data updated?

The engine can check for new SEC filings on a scheduled basis. Coverage depends on ingestion health; use the dates and original filing links to verify how recent each record is.

Can Insidir place trades for me?

Not yet. Today you can research the evidence and choose whether to trade through your own broker. Connected portfolios and permission-based automated trading are planned only for supported brokerages, after necessary approvals. No trades are placed by Insidir today.

Can I upgrade or cancel at any time?

Free research access and the $39 monthly Pro plan are listed above. Once checkout is configured, paid subscriptions can be managed or canceled in the billing portal.
