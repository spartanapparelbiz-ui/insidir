Why Insidir exists

The record is public. It is just scattered.

What a company reports, what its officers and directors trade, and who those people are sit in different filings, filed on different days, in different formats. Insidir reads them from the SEC and puts them in one research workspace, each figure linked to the document it came from.

Where the data comes from

Original sources. Checked, not claimed.

Insidir reads SEC records straight from EDGAR and links each figure back to its document. Prices and profiles come from named public sources, credited where they appear.

Insidir does

  • Show what was filed, by whom and when, with the filing one click away.
  • Keep open-market purchases and sales apart from awards, exercises and gifts.
  • Say when a figure is missing instead of estimating it.
  • Label every AI-written sentence and check it against the sources it cites.

Insidir does not

  • Place trades, hold money or connect an order to your account.
  • Tell you what to buy or sell, rate stocks or set price targets.
  • Use information that is not public. Form 4s are public reports, filed after a trade.
  • Show live prices. Prices are delayed.

Good to know

  • Reporting delay. Insiders have 2 business days to file a Form 4, and companies file reports on their own schedules.
  • Coverage. It depends on what each source publishes; the SEC notes its ticker map may be incomplete.
  • Amendments. A later Form 4/A can restate a line; Insidir counts a restated line once.

What is Insidir?

Research software for public companies. It brings SEC filings, Form 4 insider transactions and the people behind each company into one workspace, with every figure linked to its original document.

Where does the data come from?

SEC EDGAR filings, XBRL financial facts and Form 4 ownership reports, delayed prices from Yahoo Finance, and company profiles from Wikidata and Wikipedia. See Data & sources.

Is this insider information?

No. Everything on Insidir is public: a Form 4 is a report an insider files with the SEC, generally within 2 business days of a trade, and anyone can read it on EDGAR. Insidir organises those public records; it has no nonpublic information.

Does Insidir execute trades?

No. Insidir is research software. It does not place trades, hold money or make personalized investment recommendations.

What does Intelligence do?

It briefs you on new filings from the companies and people you follow, answers research questions from SEC records with numbered sources, and lets you explore institutions' historical 13F holdings with hypothetical budgets. See how Intelligence works.

How much does it cost?

$1 at checkout for a 3-day trial, then $39 a month in US dollars until you cancel. One plan, every tool. Payments run through Stripe, so Insidir never sees your card number. One trial per account.

How do I cancel?

Open Billing in your account and press Cancel subscription. It takes one step; access continues to the end of the period you paid for, and cancelling during the trial means the $39 is never charged. Deleting your account cancels the subscription first.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. Every page works on a phone, and you can add Insidir to your Home Screen from your browser's share or menu button to open it like an app.
