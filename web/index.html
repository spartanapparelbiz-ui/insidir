Frequently asked questions.

What data does insidir. include?

insidir. organizes public records in one place: SEC filings such as insider Forms 3, 4 and 5, annual and quarterly reports (10-K, 10-Q) and current reports (8-K), alongside congressional financial disclosures and company data. Every record links back to its official source.

How often is the data updated?

New SEC filings are checked every 15 minutes through the day and appear as soon as they are processed. Each record shows both when it was published and when the underlying transaction took place.

Is insidir. suitable for individual investors?

Yes. insidir. makes the same public information institutions rely on easy to search and understand. It is a research tool, not investment advice, so always do your own diligence.

Can I upgrade or cancel at any time?

Yes. Plans are month-to-month: start free, upgrade when you need more data and tools, and cancel anytime from your account.
