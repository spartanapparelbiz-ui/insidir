FREQUENTLY ASKED

Good to know.

Where does the information come from?

Insidir uses official SEC company submissions, XBRL financial facts, and imported Form 4 ownership records. Coverage may be incomplete or delayed.

Are stock prices live?

No. The public research preview currently displays reported SEC financials, not real-time trading quotes or invented market prices.

Do I need to connect my brokerage?

No. You can explore companies and build a research watchlist without connecting a brokerage account.

Can Insidir trade for me?

No. Insidir is research software and does not execute trades or make personalized investment recommendations.

Who operates Insidir?

Insidir is operated by Aethric LLC. See company information, terms, and privacy.
