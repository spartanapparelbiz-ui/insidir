How it works

From the filing to your screen.

Insidir does not predict prices or tell you what to buy. It puts the record in front of you, in order, with the source one click away.

  1. 01

    Filed

    A company files its reports with the SEC; an insider files a Form 4, generally within two business days of a trade.

  2. 02

    Published

    EDGAR publishes the original document and its structured data.

  3. 03

    Read

    Insidir reads the filing, its XBRL facts or the Form 4 XML, and keeps the link to the document.

  4. 04

    Shown

    Each figure appears with its period or trade date, its form and its filing date, one click from the source.

Insider trades, by their code.

Every line of a Form 4 carries the SEC's transaction code. Insidir labels each one and keeps open-market purchases and sales apart from awards, exercises and other events.
