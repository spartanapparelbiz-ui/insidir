The platform

Six tools. One subscription.

Each tool is built on what companies and their insiders file with the SEC, and every filing and financial figure links to the document it came from, so you can check it yourself.

See the whole platform ↗
  • 01

    Company reports

    One report per company: delayed price chart, key facts, trailing-twelve-month financials from XBRL, filings, insider activity, people and 8-K announcements.

  • 02

    SEC filings

    Search imported Form 4 filings by company or ticker, open each one with its parsed transactions and follow the link to the original document on EDGAR.

  • 03

    Insider activity

    Open-market purchases and sales kept apart from awards, exercises and other events, with the trade date shown next to the date it was published.

    Give each company a status, researching, watching or passed, and keep your notes beside the evidence. Private to your account.

How it works

Search. Read the evidence. Decide.

Insidir does not predict prices or tell you what to buy. It puts the record in front of you, in order, with the source one click away.

  1. 01

    Search a company

    Type a ticker or a name. Insidir resolves it through the SEC's official ticker map, not a fuzzy guess.

  2. 02

    Read the evidence

    Reported financials, the filings behind them and every Form 4 trade, each linked to its document on sec.gov.

  3. 03

    Decide for yourself

    Save the company, set a status and write down why. Your conclusions stay yours; Insidir never places a trade.

Questions

Good to know.

Is there a free plan or a free trial?

No. Insidir is a paid product with one plan at $39 a month. You create an account, subscribe, and the whole product opens.

What exactly do I pay?

$39 a month in US dollars, billed monthly. Payments run through Stripe, so Insidir never sees or stores your card number.

How do I cancel?

Open Billing in your account and manage the subscription in Stripe's customer portal. Deleting your account cancels the subscription first.

Where does the data come from?

SEC EDGAR filings, XBRL financial facts and Form 4 ownership reports, delayed prices from Yahoo Finance, and company profiles from Wikidata and Wikipedia. See Data & sources.

Are prices live?

No. Prices are delayed. Insidir is built for researching companies, not for timing trades.

Is Insidir a broker or an adviser?

No. Insidir is research software. It does not execute trades, hold money or make personalized investment recommendations.

Who operates Insidir?

Insidir is made and operated by Aethric LLC. See About, Terms and Privacy.
