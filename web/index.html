Why investors use Insidir

The questions worth $39 a month.

  1. What might deserve my attention?

    Purchases, clustered buying and large sales at the companies and people you follow, ranked into your briefing as research leads.

    Intelligence · Changes

  2. What are major institutional investors reporting?

    Quarter-end 13F holdings, new positions and exits, compared quarter on quarter. Filed up to 45 days later, never live trades.

    Ownership (13F)

  3. Are insiders buying or selling?

    Every Form 4 line in words: open-market purchases and sales apart from awards, gifts and tax withholding, with 10b5-1 plans marked.

    Changes · Evidence

  4. What changed at the companies I watch?

    New filings, amendments, role changes and financial results against a year earlier, delivered as they are detected.

    Autopilot

  5. Are there risks I haven't noticed?

    Large insider sales, amended filings, executive role changes and 8-K events that can change the reading of a position.

    Changes · Autopilot

  6. What evidence supports this finding?

    The SEC document, the field, the period and the value as filed, one click from every figure.

    Evidence
