QUESTIONS

Good to know.

Where does Insidir's information come from?

Imported SEC ownership disclosures and selected company submissions and XBRL financial facts. Coverage can be partial or delayed.

Do I need to connect a brokerage?

No. Research, watchlists, and manually entered holdings work without giving Insidir brokerage access.

Will Insidir trade for me?

No. Insidir currently provides research software, not trade execution or personalized investment advice.

Can I pay for Pro right now?

No. The $39 monthly plan is proposed and checkout is not active yet.
