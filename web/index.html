Changes · PepsiCo

Real filing · Oct 16, 2026

Insider share purchase disclosed

Robert C. Pohlad (Director) bought 184,646 shares of PepsiCo at $128.28 a share, $23,686,388.88 as filed. Traded Oct 14, 2026, filed Oct 16, 2026. Held indirectly, as filed: By Revocable Trust. The filing adds 2 footnotes to these lines; read them in the filing detail.

Filing detail
The four reported lines
Trade dateCodeSharesPrice
Oct 14, 2026P84,507$128.28
Oct 14, 2026P45,075$128.28
Oct 14, 2026P45,075$128.28
Oct 14, 2026P9,989$128.28

Code P: an open-market or private purchase. Footnote F1, as filed: "Represents shares of PepsiCo Common Stock acquired from irrevocable trusts established by the reporting person through the exercise of the reporting person's power to substitute assets of equivalent value for property held by such trusts…"

The footnote changes the reading: these shares moved between the director's own trusts. Insidir keeps it beside the line instead of leaving it out.
