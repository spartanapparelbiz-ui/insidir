Research with clear boundaries and no surprises.

Does Insidir place trades for me?

No. Insidir is research software, not a broker or investment adviser. It does not execute orders or provide individualized investment recommendations.

Where does the information come from?

Imported SEC Form 4 disclosures and selected SEC company submissions and XBRL financial facts. Sources are linked when available; coverage can be partial or delayed.

Do I need a brokerage account?

No. Explore research and save a watchlist without linking a brokerage. You can also enter holdings manually if you choose.

Can I subscribe to Pro today?

Not yet. $39 per month is a proposed plan. Secure checkout and any paid entitlements must be verified before subscriptions can become available.
