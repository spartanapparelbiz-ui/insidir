FREQUENT QUESTIONS

Before you begin.

What the product does today, and what it doesn't.

Is Insidir a brokerage or trading platform?

No. Insidir provides research tools and public-record information. It does not place trades or make individualized investment recommendations.

Do I need to connect a brokerage to use it?

No. Search a stock or filing immediately. Your portfolio is optional, and manual holdings are free.

Where do company and insider records come from?

Official SEC EDGAR Form 4 records and company submissions, with available SEC XBRL financial facts. Imported coverage may be partial or delayed; original filing links are provided for verification.

Is the Pro plan charging $39 today?

No. $39 per month is the proposed Pro price. Payment and access remain unavailable until the production billing flow is enabled and verified.

Who operates Insidir?

Insidir is operated by Aethric LLC. Read the Terms and Privacy Policy for important information about using the service.
