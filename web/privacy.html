1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how insidir
collects, uses, and protects your information
when you use our services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly
to us, information we collect automatically,
and information from third-party sources,
including public records.

3. How We Use Information

We use your information to provide, maintain,
and improve our services, personalize your
experience, and communicate with you.

4. Data Sources

The information in insidir comes from three kinds of source:

  • From you. When you create an account, we store your email address and a salted scrypt hash of your password, never the password itself. We also store the ticker symbols you add to your watchlist, and when you added them, so we can show related filings and alerts in the product. Alerts are calculated when you open insidir; we do not currently send email or push notifications.
  • Automatically, as you use insidir. When you sign in, we create a random session token and save it in a cookie in your browser. On our side we keep only a SHA-256 digest of that token, linked to your account, with its expiry time. Our servers also receive your IP address with each request; we use it briefly, in memory, to limit repeated sign-in and registration attempts, and we do not save it to your account.
  • From public records. Filing data originates from SEC EDGAR, the public filing system of the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission, including the ownership reports that company insiders are required to file. These records include insiders’ names and reported transactions; they come from public records, not from you.

Each record keeps a link to its original filing so you can verify it at the source. The time a filing is published is not the time the transaction took place, and amended filings can repeat earlier events. Where a feature is not yet connected to an official source, the product shows sample data and labels it as such.

We do not buy information about you from data brokers or collect it from social networks.

5. Cookies and Analytics

insidir uses a single first-party cookie, insidir_sid, to keep you signed in. It is set when you sign in or create an account, it is marked HttpOnly so scripts on the page cannot read it, and it expires after 14 days or as soon as you sign out.

We do not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies. Our pages do not load scripts, fonts, or images from other companies’ servers.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not share it with advertisers.

We rely on infrastructure providers to host the application and its database. They process data only on our behalf, to run the services.

We may disclose information when the law requires it, or when it is necessary to protect the rights and safety of our users or the services.

7. Your Choices

You can change your watchlist at any time; removing a ticker deletes it from our records. Signing out deletes your session from our servers and clears the cookie in your browser.

You can delete your account at any time. Deleting your account permanently removes your account record, every active session, and your watchlist. We keep your account information only for as long as your account exists.

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you block the session cookie, you will not be able to stay signed in.

8. Children’s Privacy

insidir is intended for adults and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you believe a child has created an account, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the product changes, for example when we add features or data sources. When we do, we will change the “Last updated” date at the top of this page.

If a change materially affects how we handle your personal information, we will tell you in the product before it takes effect.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, or want to exercise any of your choices, please contact us through the support channel listed in your account.
