How each story is built

The same three rules as the rest of Insidir.

  1. The original filing first

    Every story opens the document on sec.gov: the Form 4, the 10-K, the page the numbers come from.

  2. Every figure sourced

    Numbers are quoted as filed. Where Insidir adds or divides, the page says so next to the number.

  3. What it doesn't tell you

    A filing records what happened. Each story lists what it cannot show: motives, the future, the rest of the picture.
