Subscription and cancellation.

Effective October 18, 2026. One plan, the exact terms you agree to, and how to stop it.

1. What you pay

  • Trial: $1.00 today for 3 days of the whole product. Each account can start one trial.
  • Then: $39.00 per month in US dollars, charged when the trial ends and then every month on the same day, until you cancel.
  • No trial: if your account has had a trial or a subscription before, $39.00 is charged at checkout and then monthly.
  • Taxes, if any apply in your location, are shown by Stripe before you pay.

2. How you agree to automatic renewal

Before checkout, the subscribe page shows the exact automatic-renewal terms for your account, including the amount charged today, the monthly price, when the first monthly charge happens and how to cancel. Checkout stays disabled until you tick a box agreeing to those terms. Stripe’s checkout page repeats the terms beside the pay button.

We keep a record of what you agreed to: the terms as shown, the date and time, the offer, and the network address and browser that sent it. These records are kept for at least three years, or one year after your subscription ends if that is longer, even if you delete your account, because they are evidence of the agreement.

3. How to cancel

Online, in one step: open Billing, choose Cancel subscription and confirm. You do not need to call, email or talk to anyone. You can also cancel in Stripe’s billing portal from the same page, or by emailing hello@aethric.llc.

  • Cancelling stops all future charges.
  • During a trial, cancelling means the $39.00 charge never happens; access continues until the trial ends.
  • After that, access continues until the end of the month you already paid for. Partial months are not charged again.
  • Deleting your account cancels any subscription first.

4. Failed payments, refunds and disputes

  • If a renewal payment fails, access to the research tools pauses until the payment succeeds. You can update your card from Billing.
  • Refunds are handled case by case; email hello@aethric.llc. Any refund or cancellation right that applicable law gives you applies in addition to this policy.
  • Stripe processes all payments; Insidir never sees or stores your full card number.

5. Price changes and notices

We will not raise the price of an existing subscription without telling you first by email, at least 30 days before the change, with a way to cancel. Stripe sends payment receipts to the email on your account.

6. Questions

Billing questions: hello@aethric.llc. See also the Terms and Disclosures.
