The rules

Six rules, on every page.

Each rule is a constraint on the product, not a slogan: it decides what Insidir shows, how it labels it and what it leaves out.

  1. i.

    Original sources, one click away.

    Every filing, financial figure and insider transaction in Insidir links to the document it came from on sec.gov. Prices, profiles and headlines name their source where they appear. If you want to check a number, the way back is always there.

    In practice

    • The filing viewer links to the document on EDGAR
    • Financial values carry their form, period and filing
    • Wikipedia text is credited and linked
    • Prices are labelled delayed and credited
  2. ii.

    Evidence, not predictions.

    Insidir does not forecast prices, publish ratings or score companies. When it groups activity, such as several insiders buying in the same week, the grouping is descriptive: it says what was filed, not what will happen next.

    In practice

    • No price targets or buy and sell ratings
    • No scores, rankings or signals
    • Insider trades shown as reported, with their codes
  3. iii.

    Nothing simulated.

    No simulated prices, no sample data and no summaries or signals written by AI. Charts are drawn only from delayed provider data. When a company does not report a figure, or a source is unavailable, the page says so instead of estimating.

    In practice

    • Missing values are shown as missing
    • Calculated values show their inputs
    • No generated text about companies or people
  4. iv.

    Not a broker.

    Insidir is research software made by Aethric LLC. It does not execute trades, route orders, hold money or give personalized investment advice. Your decisions, and your orders, stay with you and your broker.

    In practice

    • A research status never places an order
    • The portfolio is typed in by you, with no brokerage login
    • Nothing in Insidir is an offer to buy or sell securities
  5. v.

    Your research stays private.

    Your watchlist, notes, statuses, holdings and followed people are private to your account, are used to provide the service as the privacy policy describes, and are deleted when you delete your account. Payments are handled by Stripe, so Insidir never sees your card number.

    In practice

    • One essential session cookie, no advertising cookies
    • Deleting your account cancels the subscription first
    • Details in the privacy policy
  6. vi.

    Honest about coverage.

    Public records are amended, delayed and sometimes incomplete. The SEC notes that its ticker list may be incomplete, not every company reports every measure in XBRL, and Insidir's filing library starts from the filings it has imported. Pages state those limits where they apply.

    In practice

    • Trade and filing dates shown side by side
    • Amendments marked, never merged
    • Every limit listed on Data & sources

Provenance

Every number has a way back.

Follow any figure back: from the page, to the filing it was reported in, to the document on the SEC's website.

  1. On the pageA figure

    A revenue line, a Form 4 trade, a filing date or a calculated fact.

  2. Its recordThe filing

    The form type, the period it covers, the date it was filed and its accession number.

  3. The sourcesec.gov

    The original document on EDGAR, exactly as the company or the insider filed it.

In and out

What you will find. What you won't.

The shortest way to describe Insidir is the list of things it refuses to show.

In Insidir

  • Original SEC filings, linked
  • Financial statements as the company reported them
  • Form 4 transactions with both of their dates
  • Delayed prices, credited to their source
  • The people who report to the SEC
  • Your own statuses and notes

Not in Insidir

  • Price targets or ratings
  • Buy or sell signals
  • Summaries written by AI
  • Simulated or sample data
  • Trade execution
  • Advertising
