Design system, v2

Calm, and exact.

One typeface, two text colours, seven sizes. Things are separated by space and one surface step, not by lines; borders are kept for inputs and table headers. Colour lives only on numbers and chart lines: green when insiders buy or a price rises, coral when they sell or it falls, gray for sales planned in advance. Every screen uses only what is on this page.

Colour

Layers and text. Muted text is the floor for contrast: 5.9:1 on the page.

Surfaces

Radius 16 for cards and 10 for controls. A card is one surface step up from the page with a 1px highlight along its top edge, and no outer shadow. Cards lift 2px on hover.

    The chart

    A 1.5px line over a fill from 16% to nothing, no axis lines, at most four muted labels per axis, a soft pulsing dot at the latest close, and trades on the line: green buys, coral sales, gray rings for 10b5-1 sales, sized by amount. Example data on this page only.

    Motion

    Springs, never slower than 400ms. Charts draw in, numbers roll to their value, new rows slide in, cards lift 2px, pages fade and rise 6px. Reduced motion: final states, no movement.
