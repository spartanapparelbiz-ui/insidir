insidir.Design system · v1

Depth, not flatness.

Three dark layers, hairline borders, a 1px highlight along the top of every card and one soft shadow. The interface is monochrome; colour is kept for data: green when insiders buy or a price rises, coral when they sell or it falls, gray for sales planned in advance. Every screen uses only what is on this page.

Colour

Layers and text. Muted text is the floor for contrast: 5.9:1 on the page.
