Partnership overview

No upfront fee and no required purchase.

  1. Compensation

    $10

    One-time commission for each new qualifying Insidir subscriber referred to a full-price paid plan. No upfront creator fee or required purchase.

  2. Referral window

    30 days

    Approved partners receive a unique link. Eligible new registrations can be attributed within the tracking window, subject to the referral terms.

  3. What we provide

    Real data.

    Product walkthroughs, public filing links and research-story ideas. You control your content and may decline to promote after the demo.

Lead with a story, not an ad.

Walk through an interesting public Form 4 disclosure. Show readers how you verify a transaction, distinguish purchases from compensation grants, and open the original SEC source document.

Insidir is a research tool, not a broker or investment adviser. Never imply that a public filing predicts price movement.

The creator kit has six ready concepts with hooks, scripts, real product footage and disclosure lines, and the research stories are public pages your viewers can open.

Program details

  • Open to creators, newsletters and educators covering stocks, company analysis and retail investing.
  • Tell us your main channel, audience and one example of your work. We will review the fit and arrange a walkthrough.
  • Tracked commission candidates require a qualifying verified Stripe payment for Insidir’s paid plan. Free signups and unpaid or refunded orders do not qualify.
  • Commissions are reviewed after a 30-day eligibility period and paid manually after confirmation. Payout details are agreed with each partner.
  • Clearly disclose your commercial relationship with Insidir in any promotional content, consistent with applicable platform and advertising rules.
