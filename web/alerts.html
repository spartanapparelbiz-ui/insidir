Phone and desktop notifications

Opt in to receive source-linked SEC filing updates for the companies and reporting people you follow. On iPhone, add Insidir to your Home Screen first. On Android or desktop, enable notifications directly when supported.

Checking device support…

Enable notifications on iPhone
  1. Tap Safari’s Share button.
  2. Choose Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Open Insidir from the new Home Screen icon.
  4. Return to Alerts and tap Enable notifications.
Android setup

Open Insidir in Chrome, tap the browser menu (⋮), then choose Install app or Add to Home screen. Open Insidir and enable notifications.
